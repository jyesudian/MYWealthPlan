MY TrustPlan

Trust-Based Financial Planning System

PROTOTYPE PORTFOLIO SUMMARY

Generated June 2026

1. Relationship Trust Scorecard

Integrates the Maister-Green-Galford trust formula: T = (Credibility + Reliability + Intimacy) / Self-Orientation Enables advisors to drag sliders to rate relationship safety and dynamically compute trustworthiness scores, providing improvement actions. Renders scorecards inside client profiles and printed reports.

2. Simulator Sandbox (Real-Time SVG)

Allows advisors and clients to live-simulate parameters (target retirement age, savings rate, yields, inflation, and budget plans) with three real-time SVG charts:

  • Projected Net Wealth Trend: An area line chart shifting colors (gold to red) when funds deplete, showing an animated warning node.
  • Milestones Timeline Ruler: Plots wedding, education, and properties.
  • Net Worth Donut: Displays assets-to-debt ratios.

3. 3-Way Aesthetic Theme Switcher

Supports immediate UI skin shifts via standard CSS Custom Properties:

  • Luxurious Dark: Premium slate and gold accents for digital tablets.
  • Professional Light: Warm cream and brown-gold text for high contrast.
  • Calming Pastel: Sage green-gray and emerald tones for approachability.

4. Custom Discovery Guides

Supports two entry processes: **Self-Guided (Client)** and **Advisor Direct Entry** modes. The Self-Guided mode includes alerts explaining *"Why we ask this"* (tax reliefs, EPF targets, LPA/Wasiat). Advisors can hover over the guide box and edit the text inline, saving changes instantly to local memory.

5. Malaysian Specific Auditing

Audits EPF retirement thresholds, verifies 10x gross earnings guidelines for Sum Assured adequacy, flags active premium payments due within 30 days, tracks Debt Service Ratio (DSR) under central bank limits, and lists local Shariah estate planning recommendations (AmanahRaya Wasiat, Takaful).

6. Luxury Print Engine

Swaps colors into a high-contrast ink-saving A4 report wrapper on print command, preserving logo headers, table borders, metric tables, and the trust scorecard section for formal meetings.
